Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Who are we?

GroenKlimaat ("we", "us") develops the Solar Panel Calculator. This application is a tool to estimate the yield of solar panels.

2. What data do we collect?

We do not collect any personal data. The Solar Panel Calculator:

  • Processes all input (number of panels, power, location, roof tilt) only locally in your browser.
  • Sends no data to our servers or third parties.
  • Uses no cookies, trackers, or analytics.
  • Stores no input outside your own device.

3. Local storage

The application may use browser cache (Service Worker) to work offline. This cache contains only the app files themselves, no personal data.

4. External resources

Public CDNs are used for displaying charts and icons (Chart.js, Font Awesome). These may see technical data such as your IP address, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. If you have questions, you can contact us at the address below.

6. Children

This application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

We may update this privacy policy. The current version will always be available on this page.
